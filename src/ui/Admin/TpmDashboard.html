1. Compliance Scoring System

  • Compliant 🟢: Time logged (>0h) or marked OOO for every past weekday in selected range.
  • Incomplete 🔴: Missing entries on one or more past weekdays. Hover badge for list of missed dates.
  • In Progress 🟡: Completed all past weekdays; range extends into today/future.
  • Exemptions: Saturday/Sunday, future dates, and OOO days are automatically excluded from penalties.

2. Workload Target Model

Target Hours = (FTE % / 100) * (Weekdays - OOO Days) * 8h
  • Balanced: Logged hours are 80% to 115% of target capacity.
  • Underburdened: Hours are < 80% (under-allocated or incomplete logs).
  • Overburdened: Hours are >= 115% (burnout warning).

Compliance Breakdown

Balanced (80% - 115%)

  • Logged weekly effort matches allocation target baseline.
  • Shows sustainable workload of 32h to 46h (based on a 1.0 FTE).

Overburdened (≥ 115%)

  • Effort significantly exceeds target baseline.
  • Indicates high burnout risk. (Logged > 46 hours on 1.0 FTE).

Underburdened (< 80%)

  • Effort is well below allocation baseline.
  • Implies bench capacity or incomplete logging (< 32h on 1.0 FTE).
