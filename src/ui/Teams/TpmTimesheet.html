TPM Timesheet Entry

Logged-in:

1. Sizing & Allocations

  • Project Sizing Predictions: Estimates predict weekly baseline effort: Small = 20 days, Medium = 40 days, Large = 60 days total. Effort is spread across the entire duration of the project.
  • PSS Tickets: Professional Services Support tickets automatically book 100% of hours to the UAT/Go-Live bucket. Excluded PSS statuses (Declined, RollBacked, Completed, Canceled) lock entry.
  • Monitoring Rule: Log events >30 mins against specific tickets. Events <30 mins roll up into general Admin.

2. Guardrails & Overrides

  • Active Whitelists: Only TPM tickets with active statuses (`Assigned`, `Preparing Deployment`, `Initiation`, `Implementation`, `TPM Handover approved`, `Go Live`, `UAT`, `Technical Go Live`) and PSS tickets with (`Flow Seen`, `Flow Pending`, `Awaiting implementation`, `New`, `4-Eye Approval`, `Review`) are active by default.
  • Hiding & Overrides: Unwhitelisted tickets are completely hidden by default to keep your grid clean (unless they have active saved hours). Toggle Show Excluded Epics to reveal and unlock them using the padlock icon on demand.
  • Phase Borders: Left-side colors show Epic phase:
    • • 🔹 Blue: Integration Phase.
    • • 🔸 Ochre: Client-Testing/UAT Phase.
    • • 🟢 Green: Completed / Live.

3. Limits & Navigation

  • Daily Limits: 1 Day JIRA baseline = 8.00h (glows Green). Hard daily cell limit is 24.00h (glows Red on over-allocation). Standard weekly goal is 40.00h.
  • Keyboard Navigation (Excel): Use keyboard arrows (`Up`, `Down`, `Left`, `Right`) and `Enter` to navigate cells rapidly. Selecting a cell highlights its contents for instant overwrite.
  • Drafts & Resets: Inputs auto-save locally. Use Reset Drafts to discard unsaved changes and reload original database data.
  • Commit: You must click Save Timesheet Entries to push drafts and OOO logs to the permanent database.
