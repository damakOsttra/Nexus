1. Sizing & Allocations

  • Project Sizing Predictions: Estimates predict weekly baseline effort: Small = 20 days, Medium = 40 days, Large = 60 days total. Effort is spread across the entire duration of the project.
  • PSS Tickets: Professional Services Support tickets automatically book 100% of hours to the UAT/Go-Live bucket. Excluded PSS statuses (Declined, RollBacked, Completed, Canceled) lock entry.
  • Monitoring Rule: Log events >30 mins against specific tickets. Events <30 mins roll up into general Admin.

2. Guardrails & Overrides

  • Active Whitelists: Only TPM tickets with active statuses (`Assigned`, `Preparing Deployment`, `Initiation`, `Implementation`, `TPM Handover approved`, `Go Live`, `UAT`, `Technical Go Live`, `Returned`, `Reopened`, `Answered`, `Retracted`, `Preparing Development`, `Completed`) and PSS tickets with (`Flow Seen`, `Flow Pending`, `Awaiting implementation`, `New`, `4-Eye Approval`, `Review`, `Completed`, `Rolled Back`) are active by default.
  • Hiding & Overrides: Unwhitelisted tickets are completely hidden by default to keep your grid clean (unless they have active saved hours). Toggle Show Excluded Epics to reveal and unlock them using the padlock icon on demand.
  • Strict Week-Locking: Weeks older than the previous week are strictly locked for editing to preserve historical data.
  • Phase Borders: Left-side colors show Epic phase:

3. Limits & Navigation

  • Daily Limits: 1 Day JIRA baseline = 8.00h. Hard daily cell limit is 24.00h (glows Red on over-allocation). Standard weekly goal is 40.00h.
  • Future Dates: Future dates are locked for logging hours, except weekends which are exempt. A 1-day timezone grace period is automatically applied for APAC users to prevent save lockouts.
  • Out of Office (OOO): Setting OOO on weekends is disabled, and weekend dates are excluded from OOO logging. Logging hours on days marked as OOO is strictly blocked.
  • Keyboard Navigation (Excel): Use keyboard \`Enter\` to navigate cells rapidly. Selecting a cell highlights its contents for instant overwrite.
  • Resets: Use Reset Grid to discard your unsaved changes and reload the last saved database entries.
  • Commit: You must click Save Timesheet Entries to push your logged hours and OOO logs to the permanent database.
  • Documentation: Access the full TPM Confluence Wiki Page for detailed usage guidelines, workflow definitions, and FAQ.
