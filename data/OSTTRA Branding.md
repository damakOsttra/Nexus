## **1\. Core Brand Identity**

OSTTRA’s brand identity is built on a high-contrast palette with specific technical requirements for digital and print use.

### **The OSTTRA Logo**

* **Color Versions**: The logo exists in only two colorways: **Rich Black** (on white backgrounds) and **White** (on black or dark backgrounds) .  
* **Usage**: Users must use professionally designed files (EPS, SVG, PNG, JPG) and are strictly prohibited from changing the logo's color .

### **Typography**

* **Brand Typeface (Professional Use)**: **BW Nista Grotesk** (Light, Regular, Medium, and Bold).  
* **MS Office Typeface (PPT, Word, Excel)**: **Neue Haas Grotesk Text Pro** (Regular and Bold).  
* **Messaging Strategy**: Headlines use "Logit Sentinel." These are provided as image files and should not be manually recreated or typed. Messaging is typically set in **UPPERCASE** with an outline, aligned left.

The OSTTRA palette is categorized into core, secondary, and accent tones.

| Color Name | Hex Code | Purpose |

| **Rubine** | \#FF0061 | Primary accent color. |
| **Rich Black** | \#222222 | Primary background and body text. |

### **Secondary and Grey Tones**

* **Ochre**: \#FE952D.  
* **Violet**: \#9125BF.  
* **Davy’s Grey**: \#555555 (used for backgrounds and body text).

### **The Brand Gradient**

The OSTTRA gradient plays a central role in brand identity, transitioning from **Ochre** (top left) to **Rubine** (center) to **Violet** (bottom right).

## **3\. Presentation Template Guidelines**

The template is designed for Google Slides and includes specific rules for consistency.

### **General Rules**

* **Backgrounds**: Use white backgrounds for slides only.  
* **Slide Styles**: When pasting slides from older decks, users must always click **"Match styles"** to ensure they conform to the current template.  
* **Footer**: Every slide includes a copyright notice: "© 2026 OSTTRA Group. All rights reserved." and the subsidiary attribution: "The home of **MarkitServ, Traiana, TriOptima & Reset**".

### **Chart and Table Design**

* **Chart Creation**: Charts should be built in the **OSTTRA Sheets Template** and then pasted into Google Slides.  
* **Color Priority**: Use the first four colors in the Google Slides palette most frequently. If more colors are needed, start with Rubine, then follow the accent palette and grey tones.  
* **Tables**: Content in tables should use Rubine for headers or accents where necessary.

### **Iconography**

OSTTRA uses specific "Solution Icons" for core business functions:

* **Connect** (Flower-like icon)  
* **Process** (Target/Circle icon)  
* **Mitigate** (Diamond icon)  
* **Reconcile** (Square/Process icon)  
* **Optimise** (Plus/Plus icon)  
* **Comply** (Linked circles icon)

## **4\. Specific Slide Categories**

The template contains pre-formatted slides for different data types:

* **Diagram Slides**: Specialized layouts for process flows and hierarchies.  
* **Map Slides**: World map outlines using brand gradient strokes.  
* **Standard Slides**: Basic layouts including title, subtitle, and private/confidential markers.  
* **Table and Chart Slides**: Structured for data-heavy presentations.
